# Planet image assets

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool for the NEBULA practice site.
Original fictional planets; visual research reference: [NASA/JPL Voyager planetary montage](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/first-and-farthest-how-the-voyagers-blazed-trails/).
The source photographs were used for visual research, not copied into the site.

## lumina

File: planet-lumina.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: realistic fictional planet catalog image for a black, navy blue and yellow space marketplace website. Primary request: A cobalt-blue ocean exoplanet inspired by the blue atmosphere of NASA Voyager Neptune photography, detailed streaks of white clouds, subtle pale cyan aurora at northern limb, natural atmospheric texture, no rings. Composition: one entire planet centered in a wide 3:2 landscape image, all edges and any rings completely within frame, subject occupying about 65 percent of width with generous margins. Backdrop: nearly black #070b13 deep space with very sparse tiny faint stars. Lighting: cinematic but scientifically plausible sunlight from upper left, shadow on lower right, richly textured sphere, NASA-inspired photographic realism. No text, watermark, border, labels, interfaces, spacecraft, collage or multiple planets. Render as a single polished original image, not an exact copy of a NASA photograph.
